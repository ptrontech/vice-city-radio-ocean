# Vitrages du Majestic

Ces deux atlas ont été créés pour Radio Océan avec l’outil de génération d’images intégré de Codex. Les trois bandes de chambres, le hall et la porte sont raccordés localement aux ouvrants du modèle d’origine. Les atlas représentent le décor visible derrière le verre. Les encadrements, la marquise et l’enseigne sont des géométries distinctes.

Les deux images sources sont [l’atlas de jour](atlas-jour.png) et [l’atlas de nuit](atlas-nuit.png). Les six fichiers BC1 accompagnent le mod pour que le joueur puisse l’installer avec Python standard. Pour recompiler les textures à partir des images, installer NumPy et Pillow dans son environnement de création, puis exécuter `python outils/compiler_vitrages.py` depuis le projet.

## Consigne du matériau de jour

Asset type: an original square production texture atlas for the glass openings of a 1930s Miami Beach art deco hotel seen in the mid-1980s. Straight-on architectural photography with a large-format lens. The ENTIRE SQUARE has exactly TWO equal-height edge-to-edge horizontal strips, meeting at the exact vertical midpoint. No margin, no surrounding facade, no stucco between frames, no perspective skew of frames, no titles or text, no labels, no people, no branding. TOP HALF: a single frontal strip of exactly three different full-size, tall hotel room sash windows side by side, each one-third the total width. Thin aged champagne bronze frames. First room with flowing ivory linen curtains drawn partly to either side and a glimpse of a dusty coral hotel interior, rounded armchair and small warmly glowing shaded table lamp. Middle room with partly lowered detailed Venetian blinds casting shadows across a slightly visible seafoam room. Third room with one curtain pulled to side, classically elegant modernist room and a brass reading light. Glass has subdued coastal sky/palm reflections while rooms remain visible. Strong real depth, selective directional warm afternoon sun, fine natural materials, subtle patina. BOTTOM HALF: a continuous street-level three-bay glazed hotel arcade looking into a real elegant art-deco lobby. At the horizontal CENTER from x=.33 to .67 is an unmistakable pair of tall dark walnut and brass framed glass entrance doors with bronze pull handles and a fanlight precisely facing the camera. On either side, broad plate-glass display windows reveal rich photographic interior detail, fluted walnut reception desk, a glowing alabaster sconce, potted kentia palm, sage velvet lounge chairs, cream terrazzo floor, shell-shaped brass motifs. Full-height bottom row in deep shade from an awning, warm light inside. Centered room perspective, clean vertical mullions. All glass, frames, and door panels fill assigned spaces, no wall gaps, no border, no printed words. Restrained vintage film colors, petrol teal, cream, sand, muted coral, natural specular brass. Physically credible photographs with fine detail in the darks. The flat atlas is the asset, not an illustration of a whole building, not a game screenshot.

## Consigne du matériau de nuit

Use case: lighting-weather. This image is a production texture atlas which is already UV-mapped in a real video game; preserve the two strips, the exact horizontal and vertical borders, every frame, furniture, silhouette, handle, fanlight, mullion, curtain opening and crop in their exact current pixel positions. Change ONLY lighting from afternoon to 10 PM in a 1980s art deco coastal hotel. A small warm amber lamp pools light on the armchair in the left upper room; the central room beyond its closed blinds is unoccupied, darker with reflected deep blue/petrol night sky; the right upper bedroom has a soft, warm lamp on. Lower street-level lobby is open for business: warm bronze sconces, gentle uplighting, warm brass and fluted wood and rich terrazzo. Lower double-door glazing and every architectural detail stays in exactly the same place. Remove daytime sunlight streaks and strong daylight palm reflections; subtle dark cobalt reflections where sky would appear. Use legible light-to-shadow gradation with visible shadows and midtones, photoreal materials, restrain highlights and bloom. No signage, people, text or border. Exact matching texture atlas, square, same two equal horizontal rows and three bays per row.
